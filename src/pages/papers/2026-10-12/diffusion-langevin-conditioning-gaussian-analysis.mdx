## Core mechanism and equation

For p=N(0,Sigma), score s(x) = - inverse(Sigma) times x. Noising changes Sigma to Sigma + tI; the effective ratio of largest to smallest eigenvalue is (largest+t)/(smallest+t), approaching one as t grows.

Under the stated schedule, Theorem 3 proves the leading W2 error asymptotic: five eighths times square root of trace(Sigma) times log(N)/N. The reported coarse upper bound scales as square root of d times the largest covariance eigenvalue, times log(N)/N.

## Authors report / AI interpretation / caveats

**Authors report:** compared with the optimized Euler Langevin baselines, the diffusion algorithm avoids the same conditioning penalty. A linear Gaussian score-learning section suggests that adding noise need not independently improve estimation in empirical covariance directions.

**AI interpretation:** the advantage resembles numerical continuation, smoothly moving from an isotropic distribution toward the difficult target.

**Caveats:** assumptions include centered Gaussian, exact score, fixed target covariance, specific discretizations and optimization choices. These do not imply a universal advantage across learned scores or generic non-Gaussian data.
